Review by hand

Compare what the OCR read with the scan, and fix what it got wrong. Transcribe exactly as printed: keep odd spellings and misprints (mark a misprint with Misprint rather than correcting it). Click any clue or square to see it on the scan at right. Everything saves in this browser as you go. When you're done, press Save review and run tools/hand.sh submit.
