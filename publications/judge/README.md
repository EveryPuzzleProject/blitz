# *Judge*

*Judge* was an American humor magazine. From November 1924 it ran a crossword
nearly every week, most of them sent in by readers; the answers appeared in a
later issue. In August 1932 it went monthly, usually with two or three
puzzles per issue. The scans come from the Internet Archive's run of the magazine.

- 509 puzzles found, 1924–1939. The magazine ran on after 1939, but the scans we have end there.
- 1924 to early 1929 were reviewed before this project started.
- **Late 1928 through 1930 are open for review now** (release `judge-1929-1930`).
- 1931 onward will open later.

`puzzles.tsv` lists every puzzle and the state of its packet.
