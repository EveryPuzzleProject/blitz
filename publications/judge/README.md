# *Judge*

<img src="../../docs/img/judge1924-11-15-cover-crossword.jpg" width="360" align="right" alt="Judge's cover for November 15, 1924: a full-page crossword grid, 'Crossword Puzzle Number', printed over an illustration of a woman">

*Judge* was an American humor magazine. Its first crossword ran on November
1, 1924, billed as the first of a weekly series of "Home Destroyers": *"The
solution of each puzzle will appear the following week. (That's to make you
buy the next issue!)"*

Two weeks later the whole cover was a crossword, "The Greatest Puzzle of
Them All", printed over the illustration (right). The week after that,
puzzle No. 4's black squares spelled JUDGE.

The crossword ran nearly every week from then on, most of the puzzles sent
in by readers. In August 1932 *Judge* went monthly, usually with two or
three puzzles an issue. The scans come from the Internet Archive's run of
the magazine.

- 509 puzzles found, 1924–1939. The magazine ran on after 1939, but the
  scans we have end there.
- 1924 to early 1929 were reviewed before this project started.
- **Late 1928 through 1930 are open for review now** (release `judge-1929-1930`).
- 1931 onward will open later.
